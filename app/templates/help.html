Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?
You can track your order from your Dashboard → Orders section or from your confirmation email’s tracking link.
How do I return an item?
Go to Returns under your account to start a return. Items should be in original condition. Our team will email you a prepaid label and next steps.
What are your lead times for bulk orders?
For in-stock items, we typically dispatch within 3–5 business days. Large or mixed-brand projects may take 1–2 weeks for consolidation. Your quote will include the ETA and any phased delivery options.
Can you install and configure devices?
Yes. We offer national installation through certified partners: mounting, cabling, conference-room setup, POS deployment, asset tagging, and on-site configuration. Email support@electrozone.com with your site list and timeline and we’ll schedule you in.
Do you offer credit terms?
We offer Net-30 terms for approved corporate customers. Apply with your business details and trade references. We also accept cards and bank transfer for upfront orders.
Where do I report an issue?
Please email support@electrozone.com with your order number, a short description, and photos/videos if applicable. For urgent matters you can also call (61) 1234-567-89. We’ll respond promptly.
